LABEL DEFINITIONS:
1. StartStop_StartFromNonMotorLane (非机动车道发车):
   • Ego vehicle starts from a stationary position on a NON-MOTOR VEHICLE LANE (非机动车道), road shoulder, or bike lane, then merges into the main traffic flow.

   • Distinct from StartFromMainRoad: the starting position is NOT on the main road but on an auxiliary/shoulder lane.

2. StartStop_EmergencyStopOnMainRoad (主路紧急停车):
   • Ego vehicle performs an EMERGENCY STOP on the main road — a sudden, unplanned stop due to an unexpected hazard.
   • Distinct from normal stops: the braking is hard and the stop is unplanned.
   • Key visual cue: ego decelerates rapidly and stops in the travel lane (not at a designated stop point).

3. StartStop_StopAtStation (站点停车):
   • Ego vehicle stops at a designated STATION or stop point — such as a bus stop, shuttle stop, or designated pickup/dropoff location.
   • The stop is at a MARKED/DESIGNATED location with infrastructure indicating it is a station.
   • Key visual cue: bus stop shelter, station signage, platform markings, or designated bay area is visible near where the ego stops.
   • Distinct from ParkRoadside: station stops have visible infrastructure (shelter, sign, platform); roadside stops do not.

4. StartStop_ParkInStructuredSpot (路侧结构化车位泊入):
   • Ego vehicle parks into a STRUCTURED PARKING SPOT on the roadside — a marked, designated parking space.
   • Includes parallel parking, angled parking, or perpendicular parking into a marked space.
   • CRITICAL: You must see PAINTED PARKING LINES (车位线) on the ground that define the parking space. Without visible painted lines, this is NOT a structured spot — use ParkRoadside instead.
   • CRITICAL: The ego must be in the PROCESS of parking (actively maneuvering into the space). If the ego is already parked and stationary, this label does not apply.
   • Key visual cue: white or yellow painted parking space lines clearly visible on the ground; ego steers and maneuvers to fit into the marked space.

5. StartStop_FollowingStop (跟车停车):
   • Ego vehicle decelerates and stops because the LEAD VEHICLE ahead stops — NOT triggered by a traffic light or parking intent.
   • CRITICAL: This label requires a visible DECELERATION PROCESS — the ego must transition from moving to stopped. If the ego is ALREADY stationary for the entire video (completely still, no movement at all), this label does NOT apply.
   • CRITICAL: This is specifically about STOPPING (following the lead to a stop), NOT about starting/departing. For the opposite scenario (ego follows the lead vehicle starting from a stop), use StartStop_FollowingStart instead.
   • The stop is reactive: the ego follows the lead vehicle's deceleration to a stop.
   • Key visual cue: a lead vehicle is visible decelerating and stopping; ego follows suit; no traffic light is the cause; the ego's speed visibly decreases to zero.
   • Distinct from TrafficLight stops: no signal involved. Distinct from EmergencyStop: the deceleration is gradual and expected.

6. StartStop_FollowingStart (跟车起步):
   • Ego vehicle starts moving from a stationary position because the LEAD VEHICLE ahead starts moving — the ego follows the lead vehicle's departure.
   • The start is reactive: the lead vehicle begins to move, and the ego follows.
   • Key visual cue: the ego was stationary; the lead vehicle ahead starts moving; the ego then accelerates to follow; no traffic light change is the direct cause.
   • Distinct from FollowingStop: FollowingStop is the process of decelerating to a stop; FollowingStart is the process of accelerating from a stop.
   • Distinct from TrafficLight starts: if the start is triggered by a traffic light turning green, use TrafficLight_*StopOrGo.

DISAMBIGUATION RULES:
D1. StartFromMainRoad vs TrafficLight_*StopOrGo:
   • If the start is triggered by a traffic light turning green, use TrafficLight_*StopOrGo. StartFromMainRoad is for non-signal-triggered departures.

D2. ParkRoadside vs ParkInStructuredSpot:
   • StructuredSpot: there are visible PAINTED LINES for the parking space. Roadside: no painted space; ego just pulls to the curb.

D3. FollowingStop vs EmergencyStopOnMainRoad vs LeadVehicleSuddenBrake (DynamicInteraction):
   • FollowingStop: gradual, expected stop behind a decelerating lead. EmergencyStop: sudden, hard braking for unexpected hazard. SuddenBrake (DI category): specifically about the LEAD vehicle's sudden braking event, not the ego's stopping behavior.
