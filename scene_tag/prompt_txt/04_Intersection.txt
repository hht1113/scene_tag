DISAMBIGUATION RULES:
D1. Protected vs Unprotected (any direction):
   • Protected (有保护) = the intersection HAS traffic lights controlling traffic. Unprotected (无保护) = the intersection has NO traffic lights at all (unsignalized). The distinction is simply: are there traffic signals at this intersection? YES → Protected, NO → Unprotected.
   • CRITICAL: These are CONTRADICTORY — a single maneuver at a single intersection CANNOT be both protected and unprotected. Check whether traffic lights exist at the intersection. If yes → Protected. If no → Unprotected.

D2. StandardUTurn vs ProtectedLeftTurn:
   • These are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE. UTurn: ~180° heading change (ego reverses direction). LeftTurn: ~90° heading change onto a perpendicular road. If the ego reverses direction, it is ALWAYS a UTurn, even at a traffic-light-controlled intersection.
   • U-turns at intersection entrance gaps (路口前豁口掉头) or within the intersection itself are ALWAYS UTurn, never LeftTurn.

D2b. LEFT turn vs RIGHT turn confusion:
   • LEFT turn: the ego's heading rotates ~90° to the LEFT after the intersection — the ego ends up on a road going to the left.
   • RIGHT turn: the ego's heading rotates ~90° to the RIGHT after the intersection — the ego ends up on a road going to the right.
   • To determine: look at the steering wheel direction and the road the ego ends up on after the maneuver. If the ego curves left and ends up facing left → LEFT turn. If the ego curves right and ends up facing right → RIGHT turn.

D3. WaitingZone types (Text vs Image vs Combined):
   • Text: Chinese characters painted on road. Image: graphic arrows/symbols painted. Combined: controlled by multiple signal phases. Check the road surface markings.

D4. TJunctionUnprotectedMerge vs UnprotectedStraight:
   • TJunction: ego is on the MINOR road merging onto the major road at a T-intersection. UnprotectedStraight: ego is on a through-road passing an unsignalized intersection. The distinction is the ego's position relative to the intersection geometry.

  Look for: cross streets, intersecting roads, traffic lights, stop lines, crosswalks.

  • Heading rotated ~180° → U-TURN (掉头).
  Only ONE of these can be true for a single maneuver. A left turn is NOT straight. A right turn is NOT straight.

Step 4 — COMBINE Step 2 + Step 3 to select the label:

  Protected + Left → ProtectedLeftTurn
  Protected + Right → ProtectedRightTurn
  Unprotected + Straight → UnprotectedStraight
  Unprotected + Left → UnprotectedLeftTurn

• Do NOT label a U-turn as "ProtectedLeftTurn" — U-turns are ~180°, left turns are ~90°.
• Do NOT output ProtectedStraight just because the ego is at a signalized intersection — verify the ego actually goes STRAIGHT (heading unchanged).
