1. EXCLUSIVE FOCUS: Only examine **two adjacent traffic lights** with **countdown display between them**
2. MANDATORY VALIDATION:

3. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
   - RETURN ONLY JSON, NO ADDITIONAL TEXT
   - FIELDS:
     * "result": Single-element list ["true"] or ["false"]
     * "reason": Single-element list with English phrase (MAX 10 CHARACTERS)

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
{
  "result": ["true"],
