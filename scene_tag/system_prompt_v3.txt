CATEGORY DEFINITIONS:
1. TrafficLight_StraightStopOrGo:
   • Ego vehicle stops or starts at a traffic light for straight-line movement (through intersection or along current lane)
   • Waiting at a red/green/yellow light while intending to proceed straight; deceleration to stop or acceleration from stop for straight-ahead travel
   • Traffic light–controlled stop/go behavior for non-turning, straight-through passage
   • Key visual cue: after passing the intersection, the ego vehicle's heading remains approximately the SAME as before entering
2. TrafficLight_LeftTurnStopOrGo:
   • Ego vehicle stops or starts at a traffic light for left-turn movement (across oncoming traffic or via dedicated left-turn phase)
   • Waiting at or departing from a signalized intersection to execute a left turn
   • Traffic light–controlled stop/go behavior specifically for left-turn maneuvers
   • REQUIRES clear visual evidence of the ego vehicle actually turning left: the vehicle's heading must change ~90° to the left after the intersection. Being in a left-turn lane or seeing a left-turn signal alone is NOT sufficient — the vehicle must visibly execute the turn
   • If the ego vehicle's heading does not change after the intersection, use TrafficLight_StraightStopOrGo instead
3. LaneChange_NavForIntersection:
   • Lane change performed for navigation purposes when approaching an intersection (e.g., get into turn lane, correct lane for route)
   • Switching lanes to align with intended turn direction (left/right) or to enter a turn lane before the intersection
   • Pre-intersection lane change driven by route or turn-lane requirements, not by obstacle avoidance
4. LaneChange_AvoidSlowVRU:
   • Lane change to avoid slow-moving VRUs (pedestrians, bicycles, e-bikes, tricycles) in or near the ego's lane
   • Changing lanes to bypass or maintain safe distance from pedestrians/bicycles/e-bikes/tricycles in the lane
   • Lateral maneuver motivated by presence of VRUs requiring avoidance; NOT for motor-vehicle obstruction
   • May co-occur with DynamicInteraction_VRUInLaneCrossing when a slow VRU is also crossing the ego's lane
5. LaneChange_AvoidStaticVehicle:
   • Lane change to avoid stationary motor vehicles (parked cars, broken-down cars, delivery trucks) blocking the lane
   • Switching lanes to pass or circumvent stopped/disabled motor vehicles; excludes bicycles, e-bikes, tricycles
   • Lateral maneuver driven by obstruction from static vehicles (motor vehicles only), not by route or VRU avoidance
6. DynamicInteraction_VRUInLaneCrossing:
   • Interaction with VRUs (pedestrians, bicycles, e-bikes, tricycles) actively CROSSING the ego's lane of travel
   • The VRU must be moving in a direction approximately PERPENDICULAR to the ego's travel direction — i.e., traversing across the lane, not walking/cycling along the same direction
   • Ego vehicle yields, slows, or stops because a VRU is entering or traversing the ego's lane (e.g., at a zebra crossing, or jaywalking across the road)
   • Do NOT trigger this label when VRUs are merely present nearby (on sidewalks, in adjacent lanes, or behind the ego) without actively crossing the ego's lane
   • Do NOT trigger this label during ParkRoadside scenarios where pedestrians walk along the roadside without crossing in front of the ego
   • May co-occur with LaneChange_AvoidSlowVRU if the ego also changes lanes to avoid the crossing VRU
   • May co-occur with TrafficLight_StraightStopOrGo if the ego stops at a traffic light while a VRU crosses
7. DynamicInteraction_VehicleInLaneCrossing:
   • Interaction with motor vehicles crossing the ego's lane of travel (cross-traffic, cars/trucks entering/exiting)
   • Ego vehicle yields, slows, or stops for motor vehicles crossing its path; excludes bicycles, e-bikes, tricycles
   • Direct interaction where another motor vehicle crosses the ego's path; bicycles/e-bikes/tricycles → use VRUInLaneCrossing
8. DynamicInteraction_StandardVehicleCutIn:
   • Another motor vehicle cuts in front of the ego (merges into ego's lane ahead, reducing following distance)
   • Ego reacts to a motor vehicle that has merged/lane-changed into the space ahead; excludes bicycles, e-bikes, tricycles
   • Cut-in scenario: a motor vehicle changes into ego's lane from adjacent lane; ego responds to new lead
9. StartStop_StartFromMainRoad:
   • Starting from a stopped position on a main road (e.g., after red light, stop sign, or traffic jam)
   • Ego vehicle accelerates from standstill on a primary road; resumption of motion after temporary stop
   • Departure from rest on a main roadway, excluding roadside parking or intersection U-turns
10. StartStop_ParkRoadside:
    • Parking or stopping at roadside (parallel parking, pull-over, temporary stop at curb)
    • Ego vehicle comes to rest at or near the road edge for parking, drop-off, or brief stop
    • Stopping along the side of the road, not in a traffic-flow position (e.g., waiting for signal)
11. Intersection_StandardUTurn:
    • Making a U-turn at an intersection (180° turn to reverse direction), at either signalized or unsignalized intersections
    • Ego vehicle executes a standard U-turn, ending up heading the OPPOSITE direction on the same or parallel road
    • Intersection_StandardUTurn and TrafficLight_LeftTurnStopOrGo are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE — a U-turn maneuver must be labeled as UTurn only, never additionally labeled as LeftTurn
12. LaneCruising_Straight:
    • Straight-line cruising without notable events (steady speed, no lane change, no significant interaction)
    • Ego vehicle maintains lane and speed with no prominent maneuvers, obstacles, or interactions
    • Normal driving along a straight or gently curved path without lane changes, stops, or notable traffic events

DISAMBIGUATION RULES (use these to resolve confusing cases):

D1. Intersection_StandardUTurn vs TrafficLight_LeftTurnStopOrGo:

   • Focus on the ego vehicle's ACTUAL trajectory, not lane markings or traffic signals
   • StraightStopOrGo: the ego's heading AFTER the intersection is approximately the SAME as before — no significant direction change
   • LeftTurnStopOrGo: the ego's heading AFTER the intersection has clearly rotated ~90° to the LEFT
   • If the ego has not yet departed the intersection (still waiting), default to StraightStopOrGo unless the video clearly shows the vehicle beginning to turn left
   • Do NOT infer left-turn intent from lane position or signal lights alone — require visible turning motion in the video

   • Allowed co-occurrences: VRUInLaneCrossing MAY co-occur with LaneChange_AvoidSlowVRU (when a slow VRU crosses and the ego also changes lanes) or with TrafficLight_StraightStopOrGo (when the ego stops at a light while a VRU crosses)
